Moves

Jump
Quick double tap. Keep the second tap down and circle to rotate.
Which jump
Forward and counter-clockwise: Axel family. Forward and clockwise: inside Axel. Backward and counter-clockwise: toe loop. Backward and clockwise: Salchow. Straight out of a clean backward landing: loop.
Toe pick
Flick down. For three seconds your next jump uses the toe pick: skating backward, counter-clockwise is a Flip and clockwise a Lutz. It goes a little higher.
Combination
Double tap again within two seconds of landing. The jumps score as one element.
Spin
Rest the finger until the ring turns solid, then circle. Left thumb down for sit, up for camel; it also lets the spin travel. The sit and the camel turn more slowly, so they are easier to place. Lift the left thumb and she lets the spin wind down, then swoops out at half speed.
Twizzles
Circle straight away. She turns as far as your finger does and keeps her speed.
Three-turn
Flick left. Swaps forward and backward skating, which changes the jump.
Split leap
Flick right.
Hockey stop
Drag both thumbs down together and lift.
Spiral
Swipe both thumbs up, then hold.
Hydroblade
Swipe both thumbs down and keep them down.
Step sequence
Flick up to make it ready, then scribble side to side. An even rhythm scores more. Each stroke is one step, to the side you stroke: short strokes are toe steps, medium are mohawks, long are serpentines.
Swing rolls
Flick up, then scribble up and down. Longer strokes make wider lobes, quicker strokes make them come faster.
Spread eagle
Spread both thumbs apart and hold. Steer it with the left thumb.
Choreography
With the left thumb steering, keep the right thumb down and moving. Slow strokes make calm, broad gestures; quick ones make sharp street-dance moves; circling spins her. The left thumb still steers and builds speed, a little more gently than usual. Lift either thumb to finish.
Speed bonus
Every move you finish leaves a little extra speed for a few seconds, and your next jump goes higher for it.
Both thumbs
Sideways flicks and scribbles drawn with both thumbs together do the same as with one.
Ways to play
Menu, Mode. Jump park: skate over a glowing pad to be launched high, then circle in the air. Race: through the green gate, collect coins, jump the hurdles, steer round the ice blocks, reach the gold gate; your time, coins and moves all count. Duet: the other skater keeps beside you and follows your moves.
Bonus
Jump out of curves and figures, link different moves within 3 seconds, and avoid repeating the same one.
