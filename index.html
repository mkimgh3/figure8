    Moves

    Jump
    Quick double tap. Keep the second tap down and circle to rotate.
    Which jump
    Forward and counter-clockwise: Axel family. Forward and clockwise: inside Axel. Backward and counter-clockwise: toe loop. Backward and clockwise: Salchow. Straight out of a clean backward landing: loop.
    Combination
    Double tap again within two seconds of landing. The jumps score as one element.
    Twizzles
    Flick up for a split leap, then circle within three seconds. She turns as far as your finger does and keeps her speed.
    Forward or backward
    In choreography, circle the right finger to turn her. Lift it, or hold it still, and she comes round to face along her path, forward or backward, whichever she is nearer. So circling her past half a turn and letting go swaps forward and backward skating, which changes the jump.
    Split leap
    Flick up.
    Toe kick
    Flick down. She picks the ice behind her, and a jump in the next two seconds goes higher; skating backward it is a toe loop.
    Hockey stop
    Drag both thumbs down together and lift.
    Spiral
    Flick right. Keep the finger down to hold it. The left thumb still steers and pushes.
    Hydroblade
    Flick left. Keep the finger down to stay low.
    Spread eagle
    Spread both thumbs apart and hold. Steer it with the left thumb.
    Choreography
    Hold or drag the right finger: anything that is not a tap or a flick. Circling starts it at once, so quick loops spin her straight away, even if your thumb leaves the glass between loops. Keep it down through a jump and she dances on from the landing; she dances until you lift it. There is nothing to trigger: let the thumbs move as the music does and she follows. Where the right thumb drifts shapes her: high on the glass she rises, reaches up and draws her leg up behind her; low she sinks into lunges, a spiral or a hydroblade; to one side she leans and turns that way. Open the thumbs apart and her arms open; bring them together and she folds in. The right thumb lifts her right arm as it moves and the left her left. Quick thumbs make her flow faster, slow ones slower. Jab the thumb in short, sharp strokes and each jab is answered by an accent, like ballet or modern dance, her upper body and her legs in turn. Her upper body moves through a phrase, arms and back together: both arms held out, one hand sliding back and lifting as her back arches; arms wrapped round herself, then unfurling. A jab takes the next step of it quickly; left alone the phrase unfolds slowly to its end. Her legs answer the way you jabbed (up: a kick, an arch; down: a plié, a lunge, a contraction). Every pose flows into the next and no two dances are the same. Circle it and she spins, half a turn for each circle, one way a camel and the other a layback: slow circles open her out into a camel, layback or attitude, fast ones pull her into a scratch spin. The left thumb still steers and builds speed. Lift the left thumb and she coasts, slowly losing speed in a spin. Lift the right thumb to finish: a spin winds down by itself, and she ends facing forward or backward along her path, whichever she is nearer. Hold the thumb still for a moment and she turns to face along her path too. Choreography is scored quietly: her moves, and more dancing or jumps within four seconds, make one combination, all artistry, added to your total when it ends with nothing shown over the dance; its moves are listed in Scores.
    Speed bonus
    Every move you finish leaves a little extra speed for a few seconds, and your next jump goes higher for it.
    Modes
    Chosen on the front page (Menu, Front page). Olympic: judged like the real thing, and a bad landing is a fall that costs points. Infinite: she never falls (a bad landing is a stumble she rides out), each score shows as one big number, and the total has no limit. Tutorial: lessons for every skill, with all of a skill's steps listed at once, and stumbles instead of falls. Each mode keeps its own score.
    Rook's stick
    Rook, the hockey player, keeps his stick in every move: on the ice in front of him as he skates, held out when his arms open, and spun over his head in a fast spin with his arms up.
    Ways to play
    Menu, Play. Jump park: skate over a glowing pad to be launched high, then circle in the air. Race: through the green gate, collect coins, jump the hurdles, steer round the ice blocks, reach the gold gate; your time, coins and moves all count. Duet: the other skater keeps beside you and follows your moves.
    Bonus
    Jump out of curves and figures, link different moves within 3 seconds, and avoid repeating the same one.
