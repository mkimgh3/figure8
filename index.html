    Moves

    Jump
    Quick double tap. Keep the second tap down and circle to rotate.
    Which jump
    Forward and counter-clockwise: Axel family. Forward and clockwise: inside Axel. Backward and counter-clockwise: toe loop. Backward and clockwise: Salchow. Straight out of a clean backward landing: loop.
    Combination
    Double tap again within two seconds of landing. The jumps score as one element.
    Twizzles
    Flick right for a split leap, then circle within three seconds. She turns as far as your finger does and keeps her speed.
    Three-turn
    Flick left. Swaps forward and backward skating, which changes the jump.
    Split leap
    Flick right.
    Hockey stop
    Drag both thumbs down together and lift.
    Spiral
    Flick up. Keep the finger down to hold it. She gets a small push going in, then glides without pushing and slows, and comes out of it when her speed runs down, so go in fast to hold it longer. The left thumb still steers.
    Hydroblade
    Flick down. Keep the finger down to stay low. Like the spiral, she glides and slows, and comes up when her speed runs down.
    Spread eagle
    Spread both thumbs apart and hold. Steer it with the left thumb.
    Choreography
    Hold or drag the right finger: anything that is not a tap or a flick. Circling starts it at once, so quick loops spin her straight away, even if your thumb leaves the glass between loops. Keep it down through a jump and she dances on from the landing; she dances until you lift it. Slow strokes make calm, broad gestures; quick ones make sharp street-dance moves. Move both thumbs the same way for long, open shapes, or against each other for twisted, closed ones. Circle it and she spins, half a turn for each circle, one way a camel and the other a layback: slow circles open her out into a camel, layback or attitude, fast ones pull her into a scratch spin. The left thumb still steers and builds speed, a little more gently than usual. Lift the left thumb and she coasts, slowly losing speed in a spin. Lift the right thumb to finish: a spin winds down by itself.
    Speed bonus
    Every move you finish leaves a little extra speed for a few seconds, and your next jump goes higher for it.
    Modes
    Chosen on the front page (Menu, Front page). Olympic: judged like the real thing, and a bad landing is a fall that costs points. Infinite: she never falls (a bad landing is a stumble she rides out), each score shows as one big number, and the total has no limit. Tutorial: lessons for every skill, with all of a skill's steps listed at once, and stumbles instead of falls. Each mode keeps its own score.
    Rook's stick
    Rook, the hockey player, keeps his stick in every move: on the ice in front of him as he skates, held out when his arms open, and spun over his head in a fast spin with his arms up.
    Ways to play
    Menu, Play. Jump park: skate over a glowing pad to be launched high, then circle in the air. Race: through the green gate, collect coins, jump the hurdles, steer round the ice blocks, reach the gold gate; your time, coins and moves all count. Duet: the other skater keeps beside you and follows your moves.
    Bonus
    Jump out of curves and figures, link different moves within 3 seconds, and avoid repeating the same one.
